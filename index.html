About

What is this

Cat Command Chat is a small private chat room. Everyone who gets in lands in the same room and sees the same conversation.

It started as a two person chat built by a friend. Its look lives on as the Orbit skin.

How to get in

The chat is invite only. If you see the invite code screen, message whoever shared the link with you and ask for the code. Type it in once and you're in on that browser.

People who join later only see messages sent after they joined, never the history before them.

To use the same account on another phone or computer, open this menu and tap Link Gmail. Then tap Sign in with Gmail on the other device.

How it works

The row of faces under the title is everyone in the chat. A green dot means they have the chat open right now. Swipe the row sideways when there are lots of people, and tap anyone to see when they joined and how active they are.

Tap your own face to set your name, pick a color for your messages, and add a profile picture.

Tap any message to react with an emoji. Tap the paperclip to share a file up to 50 MB. Web addresses become tappable links.

There is no sign up. The first visit quietly makes a guest account for that browser. Clearing site data or switching browsers makes a new guest unless the account is linked to Gmail.

Appearance and sound choices are saved on each device. The last 500 messages are shown.
