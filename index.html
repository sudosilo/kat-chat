About

What is this

Cat Command Chat is a small private chat room. Everyone who gets in lands in the same room and sees the same conversation.

It started as a two person chat built by a friend. Its look lives on as the Orbit skin.

How to get in and invite others

The chat is invite only. If you see the invite code screen, message whoever shared the link with you and ask for a code.

Members can make their own codes from the menu with Invite someone. Each code works once by default and expires. Your profile card shows who invited you.

People who join later only see messages sent after they joined.

To use the same account on another phone or computer, tap Link Gmail in this menu, then Sign in with Gmail on the other device.

Privacy and security

Everything travels encrypted over TLS, the same protection as the padlock in your browser. Someone on the same wifi sees scrambled data, not messages or pictures.

On top of that, every message is padded with random filler to a standard size, so a short reply and a long paragraph look the same on the wire. Reactions, typing signals, and notifications are padded the same way.

Files are padded with random bytes up to fixed size steps and stored under random names with no file type, so their exact size can't be used to guess what they are. Your phone trims the filler off before showing them.

What padding can't hide is timing: a watcher can still tell that something was sent and when.

How it works

The row of faces is everyone in the chat. A green dot means they have it open now. Tap anyone to see their activity. Tap your own face to set your name, color, and picture.

Tap a message to react, reply, copy, edit, or delete it. Type @ to mention someone. The magnifier searches everything you can see.

Use the paperclip for files up to 50 MB and the microphone for voice notes. Video and music links from popular sites play right in the chat.

Mention @Claude to ask the AI in the chat. Messages that mention Claude, plus recent chat it can see, are sent to Anthropic to write the reply.

Add the chat to your home screen from your browser menu and it opens like an app. Turn on Notifications in this menu to get alerts when the chat is closed, for every message or only when someone mentions you or replies to you.

Appearance, background, sound, and notification choices are saved on each device.
